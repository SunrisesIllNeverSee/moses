# Upsilon Enterprise Pilot Commercial Map

## Role of mos2es.org

mos2es.org is the **public commercial front face** for Upsilon enterprise pilots.

Its job is to convert an enterprise question into a scoped pilot engagement.

It sells Upsilon under MO§ES™ governance. It is not required to sound like the SigRank compatibility repository or public leaderboard.

## Commercial promise

> Evaluate how your people actually operate AI, benchmark the patterns that matter to your organization, test targeted interventions, and re-evaluate what changed.

Core sales language can continue to emphasize:

- Enterprise AI Operator Evaluations
- Performative Benchmarks
- Bespoke Enterprise Evals
- 30-Day Pilot
- Baseline → Benchmark → Diagnose → Intervene → Re-evaluate

## What the buyer should understand before contact

1. **What is measured**
   Operator telemetry and enterprise evaluation dimensions.

2. **What is not collected by default**
   Prompt/content inspection is not required for the base measurement layer.

3. **What the pilot produces**
   Cohort, workflow, model/tool, capability-distribution, longitudinal, and intervention analyses.

4. **What it does not claim**
   A single operator metric is not automatically employee productivity or causal business impact.

5. **What makes the pilot bespoke**
   The company selects workflows, outcomes, teams, models, and decision questions that matter to it.

## Commercial product ladder

### Level 1 — 30-Day Operator Eval Pilot

Entry diagnostic.

Outputs:
- baseline;
- cohort distribution;
- reference comparison where appropriate;
- workflow questions;
- intervention hypotheses;
- re-evaluation plan.

### Level 2 — Bespoke Evaluation Program

Adds company-specific eval design and outcome/context joins.

Outputs may include:
- role/workflow-specific benchmarks;
- controlled tool/model experiments;
- team comparisons;
- capability concentration;
- learning curves;
- intervention measurement.

### Level 3 — Continuous Enterprise Measurement

Recurring or integrated program.

Potential delivery:
- API;
- MCP;
- warehouse export;
- private dashboards/TUI;
- periodic executive reports;
- governed evaluation registry.

## Pilot modules to commercialize hardest

### AI Workforce Operating Baseline
Entry question: **What does our AI workforce actually look like?**

### Capability Distribution
Entry question: **Do we have organizational capability or a few power users?**

### Workflow Diagnostic
Entry question: **Is the constraint the operator, the tool, or the workflow?**

### AI Training Evaluation
Entry question: **Did training change how people operate AI?**

### Model / Tool Evaluation
Entry question: **What changes when we introduce Model A, Model B, or Tool X?**

### Team AI Operating Comparison
Entry question: **Why do teams using the same AI stack operate differently?**

### Meta-Pilot
Entry question: **Does this measurement relate to an outcome we already care about?**

The Meta-Pilot must keep ASSOCIATION semantics unless stronger experimental design exists.

## Public vs private terminology

The site uses Upsilon product language and polished enterprise terms even when the underlying source telemetry is SigRank-compatible.

Example:

```text
PUBLIC COMMERCIAL TERM
Capability Distribution

INTERNAL MEASUREMENT LINEAGE
SigRank-compatible source record
+ MO§ES enterprise derivations
+ cohort segmentation
+ workflow metadata
```

This is preferred to forcing the buyer to learn low-level standards language before understanding the business value.

## Primary CTA hierarchy

1. **Run a 30-Day Operator Eval**
2. **Choose a Pilot**
3. **Build a Bespoke Eval**
4. **Talk Through Your Workflow**
5. **Read Methodology**

The first three are conversion CTAs. Methodology is a trust CTA.

## Proof architecture

The commercial site should progressively accumulate:

- public methodology;
- privacy/data-minimization explanation;
- sample cohort report;
- sample executive readout;
- case study / pilot result;
- reference-field methodology;
- third-party security or methodology review;
- reproducible metric definitions where appropriate;
- evidence-backed limitations.

## Internal handoff requirement

Every sold pilot should create a scoped manifest containing:

- buyer question;
- participant count;
- observation window;
- source systems;
- privacy mode;
- selected eval families;
- selected benchmarks;
- outcome joins, if any;
- intervention targets;
- re-evaluation dates;
- decision-use restrictions.

That manifest is the bridge between marketing promise and technical execution.
